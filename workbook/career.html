One more thing

Curious about a career helping families?

Some people who use this workbook find they enjoy helping others feel calmer about money. If that sounds like you, a career in financial services might be worth a look.

What it can look like

  • Flexible hours, often from home
  • Independent work, with training and mentorship available
  • Helping families understand and protect what matters to them

What it takes: a provincial life insurance licence is required. In Canada that starts with the LLQP (Life Licence Qualification Program), a course and exam. Like any career, it takes time, learning and steady effort.
