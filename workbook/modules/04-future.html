{# ── Step 3: Beneficiary checkup ─────────────────────────────── #}
Module 4 · Step 3 of {{ m.steps | length }}
{{ ui.step("m4_s3", m.steps[2][0]) }}

A beneficiary is the person you name to receive money from an account or policy if you pass away. Old names can stay on file for years. List what you have, then check each one.

Places to look: life insurance (work or your own), RRSP, TFSA, a workplace pension or savings plan.

{{ ui.grid("m4_ben", [ ("what", "Account or policy", "wide"), ("who", "Who’s named", ""), ("checked", "Last checked", ""), ("ok", "Up to date", "check"), ], rows=6) }}

Good times to check again: marriage, separation, a new baby, or a new job. The account or policy provider can tell you how to make a change.
