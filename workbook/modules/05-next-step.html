An Experior Financial Analysis (EFA) is a no-cost, no-pressure chat about your goals. It usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes, by phone or video.

How it usually goes

1
 We talk about you Your family, your goals, and what’s on your mind about money.
2
 We look at what’s already in place Your workbook pages can make this part quick.
3
 You leave with a clearer picture Plain-English ideas to think about. Any next step is your choice, in your own time.

No cost. No obligation. No pressure. You don’t have to buy anything, and no question is too small.
