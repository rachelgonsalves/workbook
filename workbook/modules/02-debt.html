{# ── Step 2: Snowball or avalanche? ──────────────────────────── #}
Module 2 · Step 2 of {{ m.steps | length }}
{{ ui.step("m2_s2", m.steps[1][0]) }}

Two popular ways to choose which debt to focus on. With both, you keep making the minimum payment on every debt, and put any extra money on just one.

Snowball
Smallest balance first
1st
2nd
3rd

Might suit you if quick wins keep you going. Each paid-off debt is a little celebration.
Avalanche
Highest interest rate first
1st · highest rate
2nd · middle rate
3rd · lowest rate

Might suit you if you like knowing you may pay less interest overall, even if the first win takes longer.

Pictures are illustrative only. Bar length shows the size of each balance.
