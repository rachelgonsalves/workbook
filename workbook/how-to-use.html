How to use this workbook

There’s no right way to do this. Here are five ideas that make it easier.

1
 Go in any order. Start with whatever feels easiest today.
2
 Five minutes at a time. Each step shows 5 min. A phone timer can help.
3
 Round numbers are perfect. “About $400” is better than a blank box.
4
 Stop whenever you like. Look for the “Done for today?” box. It’s a good place to pause.
5
 No judgement here. This is just for you. Every ticked box counts, even one.

Two ways to fill it in

On a computer: save this file first, then open it in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader. Type in the boxes and save. (Some web browsers don’t keep your answers.)

On paper: print it and grab a pen.

Your answers stay on your own device or paper. Nothing you write here is sent to anyone.
