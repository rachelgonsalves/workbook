Privacy notice

Your information, in plain English

Last updated: {{ updated }}

This notice explains what personal information {{ c.advisor.name }} ({{ c.advisor.title }}, {{ c.advisor.agency }}) collects through this website, why, and the choices you have. It follows Canada’s privacy law for businesses, PIPEDA.

What we collect

When you fill in the sign-up form, we collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number
  • your province or territory
  • what you’re most interested in learning about, if you choose to tell us
  • whether you ticked the box to get occasional tips and updates by email

This website doesn’t use advertising trackers, and we don’t run analytics on it.

Why we collect it

  • To give you the free workbook.
  • To follow up about what you asked for. {{ c.advisor.name }} may contact you by phone or email about it. You can say no or ask us to stop at any time.
  • Only if you ticked the box: to send you occasional tips and updates by email. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To know which province you’re in, because advisors can only offer insurance services where they’re licensed.

We don’t sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Where it’s stored

The form is run by Tally, an online form service based in Belgium. Tally stores form answers on servers in the European Union, and the email alert we get for each new sign-up may pass through an email provider in the United States. This means your information may be stored or processed outside Canada, where local laws apply. A copy also reaches {{ c.advisor.name }}’s business email inbox.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the reasons above, or until you ask us to delete it. If you become a client, your file is handled under {{ c.advisor.agency }}’s record-keeping rules instead.

Your choices

  • Stop emails: click “unsubscribe” in any email, or just tell us.
  • See, fix or delete your information: email or call us (details below). We’ll reply within 30 days.
